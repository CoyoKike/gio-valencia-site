01 / Where Gio stands

Housing for all.

Homes people can afford

Affordable and public housing, more homes near transit, and protection for the tenants who already live here.

Streets for everyone

Safe bike lanes, sidewalks you can push a stroller on, and buses that come on time.

Parks and open space

Parks and wide sidewalks where kids can play and neighbors can walk, run and meet.

Neighbors at the table

Residents plan their neighborhoods from the start, in the languages they speak.

03 / About Gio

I listen first. Then we build it together.

Gio came to Boston from Colombia in 2006. Since then he has spent his days with neighbors: listening, organizing and working together on what they care about.

  • NeighborLives in West Roxbury. His kids go to Boston Public Schools.
  • OrganizerKnocks on doors and sits at kitchen tables to hear what people need.
  • PlannerHelps neighbors plan for homes they can afford and streets that work.
